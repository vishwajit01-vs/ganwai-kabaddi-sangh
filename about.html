OUR JOURNEY

History

Established 2022

Beginning of Ganwai Kabaddi Sangh

Ganwai Kabaddi Sangh was established in 2022 with the aim of promoting Kabaddi, supporting players and creating opportunities for teams to participate in organized sporting events.

Since its beginning, the Sangh has been working towards building a strong and disciplined Kabaddi community.

OUR PURPOSE

What We Stand For

Promoting Kabaddi & Supporting Players

Our focus is to promote Kabaddi at the grassroots level, encourage talented players, support teams and organize sporting events in a fair and disciplined environment.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

V

Our Vision

To create a strong Kabaddi platform where players and teams can grow, participate and showcase their talent.

M

Our Mission

To organize transparent and disciplined Kabaddi activities while providing better opportunities and support to players.
