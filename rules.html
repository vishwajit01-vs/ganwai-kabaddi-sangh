RULES & REGULATIONS

Rules of the Sangh

Clear rules and regulations help maintain fairness, discipline and transparency across the Sangh.

IMPORTANT GUIDELINES

Rules & Regulations

Choose your preferred language

01

Membership Rules

  • Applicants must provide accurate personal information during membership registration.
  • Every member must follow the rules, regulations and disciplinary guidelines of the Sangh.
  • The applicable membership fee must be paid at the time of registration.
  • Membership is personal and cannot be transferred to another person.
  • Members must maintain discipline, respect and sportsmanship during tournaments, matches and Sangh activities.
  • The Sangh may suspend or cancel membership in case of false information or serious misconduct.
  • The membership validity period and renewal process will be decided by the Sangh.
  • Membership fees and refund conditions will be governed by the payment policy approved by the Sangh.
02

Team Rules

  • Every team must complete the official registration process before participating in a tournament.
  • The team must provide correct details of the captain, manager and registered players.
  • Only registered players will be allowed to participate in official matches.
  • A team must report to the venue according to the schedule provided by the Sangh.
  • Team members must follow the instructions of match officials and organizers.
  • Any change in the registered team or player list must be approved by the Sangh.
03

Player Rules

  • Players must provide correct information during registration.
  • A player must participate only for the team for which they are officially registered.
  • Players must maintain discipline and sportsmanship during matches.
  • Players must respect opponents, teammates, officials and organizers.
  • Any form of serious misconduct may result in disciplinary action.
  • Players must follow the decisions and instructions of authorized match officials.
04

Tournament Rules

  • Teams must complete registration before the announced registration deadline.
  • Tournament entry fees must be paid according to the registration process.
  • Tournament schedules will be announced by the Sangh.
  • Teams must follow the tournament format and competition rules announced by the organizers.
  • The Sangh may make necessary schedule changes due to organizational or unavoidable circumstances.
  • The decision of authorized tournament officials regarding match proceedings will be followed.
05

Match Rules

  • Teams must report to the match venue before their scheduled match time.
  • Matches will be conducted according to the tournament format and applicable Kabaddi rules.
  • Match officials will be responsible for conducting and supervising the match.
  • Players must follow the instructions of referees and match officials.
  • Match scores and results will be recorded by the authorized officials.
  • Any official protest or dispute must be raised through the process specified by the Sangh.
06

Discipline Rules

  • All players, teams and members must maintain respectful behaviour.
  • Abusive, threatening or seriously disrespectful behaviour is not permitted.
  • Players and teams must respect match officials and tournament organizers.
  • Damage to tournament or Sangh property may lead to disciplinary action.
  • Serious violations may result in suspension, disqualification or cancellation of participation, as decided by the Sangh.
07

Payment & Refund Rules

  • All applicable fees must be paid through the payment method specified by the Sangh.
  • Registration or membership will be considered complete only after successful payment verification.
  • Applicants should keep their payment receipt or transaction details safely.
  • Refund eligibility will depend on the refund policy applicable to the particular membership or tournament.
  • Refund requests, where applicable, must be submitted within the specified period.
  • The final payment and refund policy will be determined by the Sangh and may be updated when required.
